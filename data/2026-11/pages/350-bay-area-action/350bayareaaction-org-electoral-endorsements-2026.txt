Bay Area Endorsees
In cooperation with our eight Bay Area Action local groups, our Electoral Team rigorously researches and queries up- and down-ballot candidates regarding their commitment to climate-focused action. We’ve vetted and confirmed that each candidate has also committed to not taking fossil fuel money.
