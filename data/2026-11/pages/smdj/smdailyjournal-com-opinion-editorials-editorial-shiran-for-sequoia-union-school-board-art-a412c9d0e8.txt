Editorial: Shiran for Sequoia Union school board
There has been turbulence at the Sequoia Union High School District for a while now and on a number of different topics. We believe the superintendent is trying to navigate these waters as best she can based on the divergent points of view in the school district community. The Board of Trustees is too, but there has been a lack of energy put toward communication of ideas and plans.
That doesn’t mean outreach and newsletters. That means conversations about concerns, plans and changes that have great meaning to the parents that make up a lot of the school community. For a while now, the board has had to deal with a lot of incoming communication to the detriment of providing outgoing communication and simply conversation about very important topics.
Amy Koo, the incumbent for Area A, recognizes this fact and wants to focus on building effective communication while also retaining teachers, creating safe environments and also remaining fiscally responsible. Admirable, and Koo also wants to stick with the district’s larger vision. But sometimes having a vision is not the same as sharing the vision and getting buy-in to that vision — especially if a large group of people have a different version of the vision on mind.
The fact of the matter is that there is a problem with people not feeling heard. This is where we think Alon Shiran will come in and succeed.
Shiran is not radical in his thoughts, he simply wants to bring his background in data-driven solutions to the table. He is thoughtful, practical and steady. He believes some of the discussions over eliminating advanced classes is not over, and deserves some more consideration to ensure students’ needs are being met at every level.
The high school district has faced lawsuits over alleged antisemitism and a recent decision to close a campus to save money, along with the strong opposition to recent detracking policies. The status quo could use a slight revision with a parent on the board, who can provide a voice for those who have not felt heard. Shiran’s practical approach and calm demeanor is a bonus. He deserves your vote.
In another race to represent the southern part of the district, two names will be on the ballot but current Trustee Sathvik Nori announced he is no longer seeking reelection and has endorsed his opponent Michael Dekshenieks.
