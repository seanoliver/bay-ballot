Editorial: Lipkin, Chawla, Tripathi for San Carlos school board
With two current members of the San Carlos School District Board of Trustees opting out of reelection, there are four people running for three open seats. And that includes an incumbent that was appointed in early 2025.
The candidates have varied experience and knowledge, and each seem eager to learn and serve. Current Trustee Boris Lipkin is by far the most knowledgable and engaged. He knows the issues the district is facing and is highly capable of continuing to lead. He will also provide some measure of continuity for a board that is going through some turnover.
Of the challengers, Megha Chawla and Shivani Tripathi show the most promise. We like Richard Wahl, and he could very well learn on the job, but he should likely spend more time getting to know the issues of the district first. Tripathi is intimately familiar with all the volunteer positions in which a parent can engage and is well versed in the issues the district faces. One thing that stood out with her is her ability to say she doesn’t know enough to comment on a particular issue and also her focus on data. The term data-driven decisions is becoming a bromide at times but it seems she has the ability to really use the term in exactly how it should be used. Also impressive is Chawla, who focuses on data and also making sure she can be a bridge of communication with parents. Too often, school districts rely too much on jargon and officials sometimes have difficulty in explaining the Byzantine policies they face when it comes to financing and student testing data. Gathering and explaining some of this language and these policies can only be a benefit for the entire community.
The district is facing challenging times, whether it’s enrollment, facilities or finances. Its board will benefit from leaders who understand those challenges and can take the time to communicate them in a clear and concise way. Those three leaders should be Lipkin, Chawla and Tripathi.
