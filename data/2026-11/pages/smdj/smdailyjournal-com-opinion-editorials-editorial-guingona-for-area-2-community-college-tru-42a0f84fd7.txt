Editorial: Guingona for Area 2 community college trustee
We commend Jason Holt for throwing his hat in the ring in support of faculty and staff for a seat on the San Mateo County Community College District. He is passionate about taking care of the contract needs of teachers as a way to make the experience better for students.
This is an important topic, and the heart of many discussions — including contract negotiations that are taking place right now.
Yet there are many moving parts at the community college district, and members of the Board of Trustees have myriad topics to explore in governing the administration of the three community colleges that serve San Mateo County.
Incumbent Mike Guingona has a firm grip on all those moving parts and still finds the time to explore projects like creating a women’s wrestling that have a tangible effect on the lives of those involved now and into the future. Creating that program is a true benefit, and speaks to his focus clearly on the student experience and opportunity. Guingona’s focus on making sure Skyline College in San Bruno has equitable athletic facilities squares with that effort. In fact, Guingona primarily talks about the student experience and opportunities for them to transition to four-year universities or the trades. Many community college students are the first in their family to reach higher education, and Guingona wants to ensure there is support and attention in this part of the journey for them to succeed in the next step and beyond.
Guingona has a good energy that can be infectious as he talks about what is possible through the district’s focus on free college, student housing and new facilities planned through an upcoming bond measure.
Guingona is well versed in the district’s finances, plans, challenges and opportunities. And his experience in elected office for a number of years reveals his knowledge of how best to meet any challenge and create new opportunities. He is your best choice.
