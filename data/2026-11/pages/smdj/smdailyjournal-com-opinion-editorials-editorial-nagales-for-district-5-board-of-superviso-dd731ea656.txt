Editorial: Nagales for District 5 Board of Supervisors
Two highly qualified and capable candidates are seeking the seat on the San Mateo County Board of Supervisors being vacated by David Canepa, who was elected as chief elections officer.
Both Daly City Councilmember Juslyn Manalo and South San Francisco Councilmember Mark Nagales bring political skills and experience to their candidacies and both show a comprehensive sense of the issues facing both the district and the county overall — affordability, housing, healthcare and fiscal strain. We would be well served by either.
However, Nagales brings a higher degree of public service as both an elected official and as a staff member for other elected officials such as assemblymembers Fran Pavley and Gene Mullin and former U.S. Rep. Jackie Speier, now a member of the Board of Supervisors. Most councilmembers serve on regional boards, but his service on transportation and housing boards has primed him for regional discussions on those essential topics.
He also has provided specific experience from the South San Francisco City Council on important issues like childcare and universal basic income during COVID that would tie in directly to the work of the Board of Supervisors.
Housing is a need and a critical topic for our county, by saying he would like to focus on Article 34 of the California Constitution to allow the county to find new ways to deliver affordable housing, he is showing specific plans for action.
Manalo’s experience working for the San Francisco Office of Economic Workforce Development to find innovative and new jobs in the county would be helpful, but Nagales is interested in exploring revenue generators as well, like a business license tax that would require a specific conversation with large companies that have an impact here on everyday folks.
Having difficult conversations, especially around challenging topics like tenant protections, is Nagales’ specialty and he was part of those discussions in South City so that relocation assistance in some instances was passed with support all around.
Moving the ball forward on potentially heated topics through deliberate discussions to find real results is part of Nagales’ personality. He is involved, active and engaged with anyone who seeks ways to better the community overall. He would bring that sensibility to the role as District 5 supervisor. He deserves your vote.
