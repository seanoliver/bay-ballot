Editorial: Yes on Measure K
The San Carlos School District is one of the few in the county that is adding enrollment and its state-designated funding formula continues to be in flux — meaning officials are never sure how long the current way it receives tax revenue will continue.
That means uncertainty, but also emphasizes the importance of a steady stream of locally controlled funding. That is where Measure K comes in to play. It is a slight increase to a current tax that is expiring in two years. It is currently rated at $334 per parcel but would increase to $489 per parcel every year, with annual cost of living adjustments. It currently generates about $3 million annually and while it is was intended to fund about 24 teacher positions, cost increases mean it only funds 18. Approving Measure K would allow for more teachers to be funded.
One can point to the vagaries of school funding, and how that should be reformed so that local money stays local. But that isn’t being solved right now, unfortunately. In the meantime, school officials have to seek revenue locally and Measure K is one way to ensure that class sizes stay small, teachers are attracted and retained, and students will benefit. Vote yes.
