Yes on Measure R for Burlingame schools
Burlingame schools are unmatched when it comes to their reputation for good teachers, solid administrators and opportunities for its students to excel. Unfortunately, its facilities don’t quite match that reputation, though the district has gone through extensive catchup work in a number of different areas.
The improvements made through previous bond measures are known, and needed. The most recent one, in 2020, took $97 million and focused on significant improvements at the middle school that has been bursting at the seams. Now, it is starting to look like a modern facility its students, faculty and administrators deserve.
But the needs continue. Now the district is looking for voter approval for a $100 million bond that will further improve facilities that serve more than 3,300 students across seven campuses. The funds are needed to address dry rot, leaky roofs and outdated plumbing and electrical systems at various school sites.
The district has completed over 35 facility projects since the passage of Measure O, the bond measure in , including the new Family Resource Center at McKinley Elementary School, a new transitional-kindergarten classroom building at McKinley and improvements to the District Office building. Other completed projects include the modernization of the Burlingame Intermediate School auditorium, new districtwide classroom furniture as well as various air conditioning and solar facilities throughout campuses.
In progress projects include alterations to the Main Kitchen and replacement of the fire alarm system at Franklin Elementary School and new air conditioning systems. The work must continue and the district has proven to be a good steward with public money in making improvements that make sense at all the school sites to provide safe and modern learning facilities.
Vote yes on Measure R.
