Hsieh, Richardson for South San Francisco Unified School District Board of Trustees
There are three open seats on the South San Francisco Unified School District Board of Trustees, with Amanda Anthony running unopposed in Area D and incumbents Chialin Hsieh and Mina Richardson running in areas C and E respectively. Hseih is running against parent Avin Sharma and Richardson is running against teacher and union leader Frank Lara.
While Sharma provides a good parent perspective, Hsieh holds a deep knowledge of the position and the district’s issues. She is a good listener and collaborator but doesn’t shy away from staking a position and making a decision when the time is right. Her experience in K-16 is a true asset to the district and she knows to point the ship in the right direction and be patient for all to get on board. The achievement gap is a real concern, and Hsieh was there to create the district’s 2024-29 strategic plan and revamp the math curriculum. She should be allowed to steer the ship toward success.
Richardson has been a good representative for the largely Latino Area E. She has earned the trust of her constituents and her colleagues and peers know from where she is coming. While she has not risen to board president, largely because other board members don’t want her to fill the role, she still represents her district well and deserves to continue doing so. Lara has a good and positive energy to him and could help energize participation. But he appears to know more about San Francisco, the city in which he teaches, than South San Francisco. We hope he sticks around and takes the time to be a community leader here. In the meantime, we so no reason for Richardson to abdicate her role in representing Area E. She deserves your vote.
