Editorial: Johnson for Board of Education
Beverly Gerard has immersed herself in the job in the 37 years she has been serving on the San Mateo County Board of Education. She is well versed in the duties and wants to see through policies that have been enacted in that time.
That includes the community school and court school programs, that serve a small but critical student population. Gerard is encouraged by improvements in those programs because of policy changes — it just recently became fully staffed with two special education teachers and a literacy coordinator.
Dana Johnson, challenging her for the seat, is also well versed in the duties and acknowledges improvements at the community school program. Johnson has 16 years in the Probation Department and was an adolescent treatment counselor and is currently working as the director of a family shelter supporting families receiving wraparound services. This is key experience for someone interested in making decisions on policy. This perspective will only help the board and the Office of Education overseen by the county superintendent.
Gerard is in the game, and understands the role but Johnson exhibits more creativity — including connecting incarcerated youth with college and job training programs and making new community partnerships for assistance.
Gerard exhibits passion for the position, but Johnson does too and will provide on-the-ground experience and ideas from that to improve policy for all. While Gerard deserves gratitude for her decades of service, Johnson deserves your vote.
