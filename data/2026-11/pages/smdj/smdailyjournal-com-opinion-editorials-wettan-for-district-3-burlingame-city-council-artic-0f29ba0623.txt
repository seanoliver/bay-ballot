Wettan for District 3 Burlingame City Council
The people of Burlingame have both an easy and a difficult choice. The easy part is that Howard Wettan and Jen Faber would both do a fantastic job on the City Council’s District 3 seat. The hard part is which one to choose for the position.
Both have long ties to the city in various capacities, Wettan with holding positions on two important city commissions and Faber has a school leader through the Burlingame Community for Education, one of the most important organizations in the city. Both paths have proven to be successful for current and past councilmembers in the city.
Both are in touch with the city’s issues and can describe them quickly and earnestly while providing thoughtful perspective. However, one must choose and the edge goes to Wettan.
In full disclosure, a relative of Wettan has an internship at the Daily Journal but that doesn’t factor in our decision. What does factor is Wettan’s deeper understanding of the city’s issues including its budget, economic opportunities, maintaining character amid growth, and traffic. As a member of the Traffic, Safety and Parking Commission, Wettan knows the city’s needs to keep the streets safe for all through thoughtful decisions that don’t pit people against each other but rather look for wins for all. As a member of the Parks and Recreation Commission, Wettan didn’t shy from controversial topics and found solutions that benefited Burlingame. It is that type of leadership, through listening, learning then acting, that makes Wettan appealing in his candidacy. It is clear he is a natural leader, and a smart, amicable and decisive one at that. And his knowledge of the city’s varied issues is deeper and hold a higher level of specificity. He would truly hit the ground running.
Faber would also be a good fit for this position, and it’s clear she could learn quickly.
Yet voters must choose and the best choice is Wettan.
