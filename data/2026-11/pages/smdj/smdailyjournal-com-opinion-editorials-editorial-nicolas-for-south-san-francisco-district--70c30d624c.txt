Editorial: Nicolas for South San Francisco District 3
Flor Nicolas may not be the most progressive member of the South San Francisco City Council, but she listens to constituents, puts thought into her decisions and responds and votes accordingly.
District elections were intended to put elected officials more in touch with their constituents and Nicolas has proven she has her ear to the ground and responds accordingly. As an incumbent, she also participates in serious discussions that affect the entire city and is thoughtful in her ideas such as doing more multiyear budgeting to reduce financial surprises and keeping its pool of reserves strong.
She has deep roots in the city, but so do her competitors, Melanie Olson and Sophia Cyris. Olson has a good handle on the issues facing the city and would be a great choice if Nicolas wasn’t the best candidate for this position. Cyris provides a fresh energy and perspective and will likely grow into a better candidate in years to come as she learns more details about the city’s governance.
Olson is a member of the Parks and Recreation Commission, but she seems to be a natural fit for the Planning Commission as she has experience as a city planner for the town of Woodside. Some of her ideas, including using flex zoning, could find a home in the discussions often heard at the Planning Commission.
Nicolas has a practical stance when it comes to city issues, and the city overall benefits from her know-how, perspective and experience. She is a good representative for the city, and deserves your vote.
