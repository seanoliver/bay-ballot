Editorial: Schneider, Yam for Millbrae City Council
Former Millbrae Mayor Ann Schneider can rub people the wrong way, but she also knows her stuff. The same cannot be said about her opponent in the District 3 race for Millbrae City Council. Candidate Juan Gamez is pleasant, but out of his depth. While he can give a surface level summary of the city’s issues, it is Schneider who can give it a deep dive — whether you asked for it or not. More importantly, Schneider can provide some historical context to issues in the city and bring some institutional knowledge to a council that is sometimes lacking. She would provide some depth on the dais. Politics can get contentious and sometimes Schneider does herself no favors and takes things too personally when it’s just business. If she can contend with those spirits and focus on the welfare of Millbrae’s residents, she just might make some of the progress she desperately seeks. Vote Schneider.
In the District 4 race, appointed incumbent Bob Nguyen is facing off against Albert Yam. Both have a decent grip on the city’s issues. When it comes to downtown, which is critical to Millbrae’s character and success, Yam has better defined ideas that are in tune with the city’s small businesses. He wants to support more business diversity, expedite the permit process and connect travelers to the commercial corridor. These are specific and strong ideas. Nguyen is service oriented, which is good, but Yam is action oriented, which is better. Vote Yam.
