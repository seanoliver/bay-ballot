Editorial: Venkat for District 1 Foster City Council
Two current councilmembers, Mayor Art Kiesel and Councilmember Phoebe Venkat, are vying for the same District 1 seat this November as the city begins voting by district rather than at large.
Kiesel is a council mainstay, with a steady-as-she-goes attitude and a keen sense of civic duty. He is familiar with the city and its history, and its residents benefit from his know-how. Venkat is a work in progress, with a high level of energy and creative thinking. She is up to speed and willing to bring up issues others may not, and is always willing to fight for the little guy.
Foster City is not known for a progressive approach to any matter, and sometimes it feels a bit exclusionary. It was carved out of marshland to be a bedroom community more than and did its part to add housing to the region. But much has changed in that time, and with that change, priorities and discussions must change.
Venkat speaks up and speaks out. When the city sparked dissent over the use of a building for the county’s Human Services Agency, it was Venkat who sought truth over misinformation about its use and remained steadfast in support of a facility change to serve people in the community.
In her time on the council, she has been highly communicative and transparent. That is refreshing for a city that at times can feel closed off.
We are disappointed in her stance against any traffic flow improvements for State Route 92 and Highway 101 and feel she hasn’t truly thought about how sometimes multiple efforts of different types can help solve problems rather than just looking at an issue with a single lens. However, she does appear open to other points of view at times and we hope she will consider that sometimes it can be practical to explore a variety of solutions.
Overall, Kiesel has served the city well and typically would deserve another four years on the council. However, district elections sometimes forces two capable councilmembers to compete for one seat. That is the case in this instance. Venkat has proven to be a quick learner and her ability to communicate and bring a needed perspective to the city gives her the edge in this contest. She deserves your support.
