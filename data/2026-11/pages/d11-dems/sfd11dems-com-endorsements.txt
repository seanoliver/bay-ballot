Important Endorsement Process information:
Please note - in order to receive an endorsement ballot and have your vote be counted, club members must attend the endorsement meeting (be logged into zoom and confirm attendance), and be in good standing.
If you are a continuing member, please be sure to renew club dues prior to the meeting, no later than at Noon, if you have not already done so. Pay your club membership dues here!
New members must have paid their club dues not later that 30 days prior to the endorsement meeting (i.e., or before)
Per Article 4 Section 6 of our bylaws a candidate must receive 60% of votes cast in order to earn an endorsement, and ballot measures must receive 50%+1 of votes cast. If the prescribed threshold is not reached then "No Position" will be identified.
Our club bylaw can be viewed here.
