OUR ENDORSEMENTS
To qualify for consideration for endorsement by SBPA, candidates must make the following 4 affirmative pledges.
CORPORATE-FREE. I commit to be a corporate-free candidate, and publicize that I am a corporate free candidate in my campaign literature.
PLEDGE OF ACCOUNTABILITY. I commit to publicize and hold regular listening sessions with my constituents, to stay in touch with their needs and concerns.
PROGRESSIVE POLICIES. I commit to author, co-author, support, promote, and defend people-focused progressive policies and practices that incorporate SBPA core values to improve communities and the quality of life for my constituents.
DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION. I commit to help level the playing field for ALL people, and prioritize
the development of leadership among under-represented communities.
