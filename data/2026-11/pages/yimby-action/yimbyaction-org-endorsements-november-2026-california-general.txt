How our endorsements work
We endorse candidates, ballot measures, and legislation. YIMBY members vote on every endorsement.
01
Chapters do the research
Local members study the races, send questionnaires, and interview candidates about housing.
02
Members vote
Every endorsement — candidate, ballot measure, or bill — is decided by a vote of YIMBY Action members, not staff.
Read our full procedures
03
We put it to work
Endorsements go into voter guides, and chapters knock doors and phone-bank for the slate.
Voter guides are member funded
Members shape who we endorse and keep this research free.
