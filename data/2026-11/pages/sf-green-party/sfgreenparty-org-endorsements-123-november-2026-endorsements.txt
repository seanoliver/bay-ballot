[voter guide front side] These are the SF Green Party's endorsements for the election. A complete explanation of all our endorsements will be in our Green Voter Guide to be published here.
SF Candidates:
Congress: Connie Chan (endorsed in the primary)
Supervisor, D4: Jeremy Greco
Supervisor, D8: Michael Nguyen
Supervisor, D10: Pearci "PJ" Bastiany III
School Board: Virginia Cheung and Alida Fisher
College Board: Leah LaCroix and Jeremy Lee
SF Ballot Props:
NO on A - commission reform to lower public oversight
YES on B - keep moving towards a public bank
no consensus for or against C - housing trust fund
NO on D - restricting ballot access for future propositions
NO on E - less oversight over City contracting
NO on F - more unelected, corrupt appointees and less accountability
no endorsement for or against G - the measure to open the Great Highway to cars on weekdays
YES on H - progressive parcel tax to fund Muni
no consensus for or against I - dedicate $120 million from real estate transfer tax to "affordable housing"
YES on J - end exemption to real estate transfer tax by banks foreclosing on properties
no consensus for or against RTM - regional sales taxes to fund BART, Muni, and other local transit
CA Ballot Props:
NO on 1 - bond to fund private housing for veterans
NO on 2 - put more state tax money in "rainy day fund" instead of priorities like public transit
YES on 3 - keep the tax on rich people to fund public education
YES on 4 - allow public financing of candidates for state office
NO on 5 - neuter state recall elections instead of reforming them using RCV
NO on 37 - $25 billion bond for subsidized mortgages for new housing
NO on 38 - $8.4 billion bond for immunology research
NO on 39 - make it harder to vote by requiring people to show ID or write it on mail-in ballot
YES on 40 - billionaire wealth tax (we helped gather sigs for this)
NO on 41 - makes it harder to raise taxes
NO on 42 - blocks billionaire tax
NO on 43 - makes it harder to raise local taxes (2/3 requirement instead of majority)
YES on 44 - SEIU-UHW measure to require more of health clinic revenues to go to services instead of executive pay
NO on 45 - guts environmental protection laws in order to enable data centers and other development
