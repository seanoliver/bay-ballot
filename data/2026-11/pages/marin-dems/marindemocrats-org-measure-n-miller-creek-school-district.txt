Measure N - Miller Creek School District
[Marin Dems Logo]
[Measure N Logo]
Vote YES on Measure N to repair and upgrade aging Miller Creek schools and support quality education for students.
Most MCSD schools were built 60 to and need repairs and updates to meet current academic and safety standards.
Vote YES on N to:
Fix deteriorating bathrooms, plumbing, roofs, HVAC, and electrical systems
Upgrade classrooms for math, science, technology, reading, writing, arts, and music
Improve school security, locks, lighting, emergency communications, and cameras
Make classrooms, playgrounds, and campuses accessible for students with disabilities
All funds benefit local schools only:
No funds can be taken by state or federal governments
No funds used for administrators’ salaries or benefits
Independent oversight, annual audits, and public spending disclosures
Join parents, teachers, and community leaders in voting YES on N for safe and updated Miller Creek schools!
To donate click here: https://yesonnformillercreekschools.org/donate
