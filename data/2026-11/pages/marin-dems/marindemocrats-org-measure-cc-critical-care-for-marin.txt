Measure CC- Critical Care For Marin
[Marin Dems Logo]
[Measure CC logo]
Vote YES on Measure CC, to protect local access to life-saving emergency care and hospital services close to home by keeping MarinHealth staffed, equipped, and ready! For victims of heart attacks, strokes, and accidents, seconds mean the difference between life and death.
Measure CC ensures that we have access to life-saving medical care when we need it most. In Marin County, access to emergency care is especially critical — we have only one major freeway, and traffic delays to the nearest hospital in San Francisco or Sonoma can exceed an hour. MarinHealth Medical Center is the only designated trauma center in Marin County — and a full-service community hospital providing emergency care, heart and stroke treatment, cancer care, surgery, and mental health services. Studies show that trauma center care can increase survival rates by up to 25%.
Measure CC is proudly endorsed by local doctors and nurses, Congressman Jared Huffman, Assemblymember Damon Connolly, the Marin County League of Women Voters, the Marin Democratic Party, Marin Professional Firefighters, the North Bay Labor Council, and more!
When an emergency strikes, we need quick access to excellent medical care. Join local first responders and doctors who rely on MarinHealth every day to save lives. Vote YES on Measure CC!
Learn more about our endorsements and the measure at friendsofmarinhealth.org/endorse
