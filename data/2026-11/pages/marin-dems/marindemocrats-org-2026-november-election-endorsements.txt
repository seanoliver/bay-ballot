[Jared Huffman]
Re-Elect Jared Huffman, Congressional District 2
Families are being squeezed by skyrocketing costs of living, while the President enriches himself and stokes chaos and culture wars. A healthcare crisis looms because of drastic cuts to Medicaid and Medicare in last year’s partisan tax giveaway for billionaires. A compliant GOP Congress refuses to challenge the President’s reckless actions at home and abroad, including the lawless brutality of ICE. Our democracy is under siege.
You deserve better.
Congressman Huffman is fighting for lower costs, accountability, and a livable future, while producing tangible improvements for our communities -- including millions in new funding for local health clinics, affordable housing, and water supply and flood control projects.
Representing this vast district from the Golden Gate Bridge to the Oregon and Nevada borders is a big job. For fourteen years, Jared has driven the long roads, shown up in every community, listened, and delivered results while staying true to our shared values.
As Ranking Member of the Natural Resources Committee, Jared is leading the fight against offshore‑drilling, defending our public lands, and working to improve wildfire resilience. Jared has partnered across the aisle when it helped our communities, including securing increased special‑education funding. He co‑sponsored legislation forcing transparency and accountability in the Epstein coverup, and is leading efforts to ban members of Congress from owning and trading stocks.
Congressman Huffman’s commitment is to always lead with clarity, courage, and solutions—to defend democracy, confront corruption, protect our planet, and get things done even when politics are tough.
Learn more: https://www.jaredhuffman.com
[Damon Connolly]
Damon Connolly, State Senate District 2
I’ve spent the last 25 years advancing Democratic values, previously serving as a Marin County Supervisor, School Board President, and Vice Mayor. I’m running for State Senate because we need determined, proven leadership to navigate these challenging times.
My signature achievements in the State Assembly include a landmark climate bond that invested billions in wildfire and drought mitigation, reforms that make air cleaner near schools and daycares, and major local investments in affordable housing. I also authored a wildfire home insurance law, AB 1, that will deliver relief to homeowners who invest in home hardening. I’m currently working on legislation to clamp down on corporate tax loopholes that benefit the world’s largest corporations at California taxpayers' expense.
My priorities are North Bay priorities: climate action, health care affordability, reproductive rights, smart solutions to address our housing and homelessness crises, and protections for vulnerable communities, including immigrants.
Helping those in need and preserving the things that make our region and state a beacon of hope in these dark times are what compel me to service.
This visionary campaign is unifying the North Bay and North Coast. I’m proud to be endorsed by U.S. Senator Adam Schiff, Attorney General Rob Bonta, Congressmembers Jared Huffman and Mike Thompson, State Senator Mike McGuire, Assemblymember Chris Rogers, Sierra Club, California Environmental Voters, California Professional Firefighters, California Nurses Association, National Union of Healthcare Workers, California Teachers Association, SEIU California, every Marin County Supervisor, Marin County’s District Attorney, Sheriff, and Superintendent of Schools.
Learn More: Damon Connolly for State Senate

[Bruce Ackerman]
Bruce Ackerman - Fairfax Town Clerk
I am a former Mayor and long time volunteer for our Town. Over my 33
years in Fairfax the Clerk election has been uncontested. This year it is
contested, and there are differences between the candidates.
In the few California towns and cities in which the position hasn’t been
eliminated, the unpaid Elected Clerk deputizes the paid professional staff
who do most of the complex work of the Clerk’s Office.
The elected Clerk has limited powers – but could still potentially create
disarray for the Town government’s effective and efficient operations –
even while a new Town Manager is being hired.
I am committed to doing what I can to maintain orderly Town functioning
and insure that the work of staff, such as administering fair elections and
complying with state law, continues. I would also keep citizens informed of
what’s happening in Town Hall.
Learn more at: https://www.bruceackermanforfairfax.com/

[Steve Burdo]
Steve Burdo - San Anselmo Town Council
Steve Burdo is the Mayor of San Anselmo and has been a member of the Town Council since 2019.
During his time on the Town Council, Steve has helped lead the transformation of San Anselmo from a sleepy town to one of the most vibrant places in Marin County. Leading with his democratic and environmental values, and through a willingness to try new ideas, Steve has been part of many of San Anselmo’s recent accomplishments, including: Creating Marin County’s first parklet program during the Covid-19 pandemic, changing San Anselmo’s downtown into a gathering space through the Town’s Live on the Avenue Summer Event Series, and creating the Ross Valley’s first solar resiliency hub at Town Hall.
Steve has also served as the President of the Ross Valley Fire Department Board of Directors where he helped lead the efforts to achieve full staffing for the first time in the Department’s history.
[Cristine DeBerry]
Cristine Soto DeBerry - Southern Marin Fire Protection District
Cristine DeBerry has spent more than 25 years in public service, including as Deputy Chief of Staff to San Francisco Mayor Gavin Newsom and a decade as Chief of Staff to the San Francisco District Attorney, where she helped guide the city's budget, labor negotiations, and public safety policy. She brought that experience home to Marin in 2019, when she was appointed to the Southern Marin Fire Protection District board; voters have since re-elected her in 2020 and 2022.
On the board, Cristine has served as President, Vice President, and Chair of the Emergency & Disaster Preparedness Committee. Under her leadership, the district merged the Mill Valley Fire Department without raising taxes, completed wildfire inspections of every home in the wildland-urban interface, and earned state recognition for meeting California's highest wildfire-prevention standards — all while maintaining a balanced budget with healthy reserves. Cristine lives in Mill Valley with her husband and two children.
