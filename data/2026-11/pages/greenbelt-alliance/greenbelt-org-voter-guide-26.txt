Voter Guide
As federal protections for land, climate, and the environment continue to erode, locally driven policies have never mattered more. California and the Bay Area are stepping up, and the election this November will be decisive for CEQA, housing, environment, and climate issues.
Greenbelt Alliance’s team of experts assessed measures across the region and multiple opportunities to reinforce your commitment to climate resilience, smart housing, and open spaces.
On , you don’t have to hesitate to vote for climate resilience. We’ve got your back.
