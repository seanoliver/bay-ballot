Welcome
The Sonoma County Asian Pacific Islander ("API") Democratic Club was formed to foster an active interest in governmental affairs among API Democrats, to promote the interests of APIs within Sonoma County, and to ensure the equal political and institutional participation in the Sonoma County Democratic Party. Our members live in the communities of Kenwood, Petaluma, Santa Rosa, Sebastopol, Sonoma, unincorporated Sonoma County, and Windsor.
