Napa County Registrar of Voters, Roster of Candidates, General Election 11/3/2026 (contests 3110 to 5501, qualified candidates only, printed 8/13/2026).
Source: https://www.napacounty.gov/DocumentCenter/View/46087/November-3-2026---List-of-Qualified-Candidates (linked from https://www.napacounty.gov/2328/Qualified-List-of-Candidates; retrieved 2026-10-09 in a browser, since napacounty.gov returns 403 to curl)
Redacted extract: contest number and name, seats, "On Ballot", and each candidate's name as the county prints it (in capitals). A ballot designation of "Incumbent" or "Appointed Incumbent" is kept; other designations, mailing addresses and contact details are omitted.
The roster covers local contests only. Congress District 4, Assembly District 4, the Board of Equalization, statewide offices and the court retentions are on the SoS certified list (CA-Certified-Candidates-Nov2026.pdf). The June 2, 2026 primary results (https://www.napacounty.gov/4186/June-2-2026-Election-Results) show Napa has one contest each for Congress (District 4), Assembly (District 4) and Board of Equalization (District 2), and no State Senate contest.
"On Ballot: No" contests had no more candidates than seats and are not printed.
