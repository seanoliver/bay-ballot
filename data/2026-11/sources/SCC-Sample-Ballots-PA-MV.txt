Extract: precincts sampled, and each ballot style's district, county, city and regional contests and measures, with candidates and ballot designations. Statewide offices and propositions are the same on every style and omitted.
Precinct 0002010 (Ballot Type 2) adds Saratoga-area school contests and Los Gatos-Saratoga Measure N to the Palo Alto ballot. Unverified: the registrar publishes this style, but the precinct's voter count is unknown. No guide takes a position on those school races, and Measure N is not on Bay Ballot because no other Palo Alto voter sees it.
Precinct 0002151 (Ballot Type 7) adds Fremont Union High School District Trustee Area 3 to the Palo Alto ballot. Unverified: the registrar publishes this style, but the precinct's voter count is unknown. The race is on Bay Ballot because a guide (Santa Clara County Democratic Party) takes a position on it.
