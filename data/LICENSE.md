# Data license

The data in this directory is licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-4.0/) (CC BY 4.0), with the exceptions below. Credit it as "Bay Ballot (bayballot.com)".

This covers the ballot, area, guide and changelog files, and the picks in each endorsement file.

It does not cover:

- **Quotes.** The `quotes` in endorsement files are copied word for word from each voter guide. They belong to the guide that wrote them.
- **Archived pages.** `<election>/pages/` holds copies of guides' published pages. They belong to each guide.
- **Source documents.** `<election>/sources/` holds documents published by election offices and others, under their own terms.
