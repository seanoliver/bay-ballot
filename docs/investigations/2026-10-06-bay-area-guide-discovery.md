# Bay Ballot: Bay Area expansion discovery (Nov 3, 2026)

Researched 2026-10-06. "Published" means a Nov 2026 page was confirmed live on 2026-10-06, either by fetching it directly or, where the site blocks bots, by a dated secondary source that I name. "Unverified" means I could not confirm it. No quotes are used.

**Caveats on access.** Several sites block automated fetches with 403 errors or Incapsula bot protection:
- sierraclub.org
- acdems.org
- vote.santaclaracounty.gov
- marincounty.gov
- napacounty.gov
- lwvoakland.org
- bluevoterguide.org

For those, the status comes from search snippets or from Oaklandside's Oct 5 guide-to-guides, and is marked that way. Newspaper pages with picks are paywalled, so I confirmed their headlines and URLs but not their full text.

Population figures are approximate (2020 Census, rounded).

## Recommendation: launch Alameda, then Santa Clara, then Sonoma (San Mateo as alternate)

1. **Alameda (launch first).** It has the most guides by far: about 17 published, plus a ready-made index of them from Oaklandside. It also has the most guides that explain their picks (EB4E, Oakland Rising, Empower Oakland, SPUR, the Leagues). The population is large (1.65M) and the electorate is the most guide-reading in the region. It shares RTM with SF, and SPUR, the Chronicle, SEIU 1021 and the Sierra Club records already exist in the SF data. The cost is ballot size: 13–14 cities and 20+ measures. Mitigation: launch with Oakland, Berkeley, Alameda, Emeryville and San Leandro plus the regional seats (BART, AC Transit, EBMUD), where nearly all the guides concentrate. Then add Fremont, Hayward and the Tri-Valley cities.
2. **Santa Clara (second, for population).** It is the largest county (1.93M). Party, labor, LCV, YIMBY, Sierra Club and SV@Home are all published. The contests are fairly contained: San Jose has only three council runoffs (D5/D7/D9), and the measures are mostly small (Mountain View, Palo Alto, Los Altos, Cupertino) plus the shared RTM. Weaknesses: most guides are list-only, the Mercury News hasn't published local picks yet (expect them mid-to-late October), and the registrar site blocks scraping.
3. **Sonoma (third, for low effort and high quality).** The Press Democrat keeps a single running roundup page with explained picks. With the county Democrats and the North Bay Labor Council, that already covers most contests. The ballot is moderate (8–9 cities, 14 measures), and the population is about 485K.
   - **Alternate: San Mateo.** It has the best machine-readable registrar data (roster PDF; 18 cities), is next to SF, and shares RTM. But its party slate isn't posted yet and its labor slate is Word docs. Switch to San Mateo if SMCDems posts its picks in the next week.

**Defer:**
- **Contra Costa:** Only 2 local guides so far, and the East Bay Times hasn't done local picks yet. Revisit after the EBT local picks come out.
- **Marin:** Good coverage and explained picks (the IJ), but small (260K) and heavy on measures (32).
- **Napa:** The newspaper dropped endorsements, and the ballot is small.
- **Solano:** No guides that explain their picks, and the newspapers are unverified.

**Cross-county leverage:** SEIU 1021 (8 of 8 counties on one page), Greenbelt (7), Bay Rising (5), and the Sierra Club chapters (SF Bay plus Loma Prieta, 6 counties) can each be ingested once and fanned out by county.
